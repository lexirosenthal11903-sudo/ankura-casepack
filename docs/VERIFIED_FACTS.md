| Which complaint clock applies to a fraud or scam complaint | **15 business days, not 8 weeks.** "If a customer complains to you about the way your firm handled a fraud or scam, you must reply within 15 business days. According to the FCA's dispute resolution rules (DISP) 1.6.2A, this should be either with a final response, or to indicate why you can't yet answer the complaint and telling them when you will, which must be within 35 business days." The answer key had assumed 8 weeks for APP scams. Corrected in prompt v4, policy CP-3.2 and the keys for HV-001, HV-016 and HV-021 on 11 Nov 2026, after the blind held-back run. | financial-ombudsman.org.uk/businesses/resolving-complaint/complaints-deal/fraud-scams (fetched 11 Nov 2026) |
